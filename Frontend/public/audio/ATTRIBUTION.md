# Sanctuary ambience audio

These recordings are bundled locally so playback does not depend on third-party hosting.

- `rain-loop.mp3` — “AMB Rain Loop 1” by Kresiek The Furry, CC0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/amb-rain-loop-1
- `wind-in-tree.mp3` — “Wind in a Tree” by Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, CC0. Source: https://bigsoundbank.com/wind-in-a-tree-s0659.html
- `birds-forest.ogg` — “Birds forest” by Barracuda1983, released to the public domain. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Birds_forest.ogg
- `nature-rain-birds.ogg` — “Rainthunderandbirds” by Ezwa, released to the public domain. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rainthunderandbirds.ogg
- `sea-waves.mp3` — “Sea Waves” (#698) by Joseph SARDIN, CC0. Source: https://bigsoundbank.com/sea-waves-s0698.html
- `fireplace.mp3` — “Fireplace #1” (#30) by Joseph SARDIN, CC0. Source: https://bigsoundbank.com/fireplace-1-s0030.html

All six recordings permit reuse without attribution. Credits are included here as a courtesy and for license traceability. White, pink-style, and brown noise are generated locally with the Web Audio API and are not recordings.
